Linear Regression

Used for: Regression

How it works:

  • Fits a "best-fit" straight line (or hyperplane) through data points
  • Relationship between independent variables (X) and a continuous dependent variable (Y).

Use Cases:

  • Predicting house prices based on square footage and location
  • Forecasting future sales revenue based on ad spend
  • Estimating the impact of different factors on a continuous outcome
