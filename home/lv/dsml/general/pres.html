You can use AI to provide a template for the chosen presentation topic and further prompts to refine it.

Please, keep the following in mind when creating the slides:

  • Provide a table of contents at the beginning
  • Provide a meaningful structure
  • Keep text concise and to the point
  • Don't overcrowd slides with information
  • Especially, avoid excessive text
  • Use visuals to enhance understanding, not because they look nice
  • Don't just collect a bunch of pictures from the internet
  • Make sure visuals are relevant and add value
  • Make sure you understand the visuals you include
  • Make sure you understand the contents you present
